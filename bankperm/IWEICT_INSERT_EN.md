# IWEICT insert: channel order at w = 4

**Where.** In `iweict2026/PAPER_EN.md`, Section V (Results), as a new paragraph directly after the paragraph that begins "**Board.** The maxout layers (K = 288, 64 outputs) took 289 cycles per window" and before the line "*Table II. No existing number moves: every reference [1]–[23] is first cited before this point, so the list stays in order of first citation.

**Text (three sentences).**

**Channel order at w = 4.** At w = 4 the compute term of (1) matters: in sparse mode a window waits for the fullest of the four tap banks, and as tap t goes to bank t mod 4 in windows ordered (ky, kx, channel), each bank of a 32-channel layer holds the channels whose index is the same mod 4, like the lanes of Cnvlutin [24]. Relabelling the B-32 ReLU network's hidden channels offline, folded into the adjacent layers' weights [25] so that the output stays bit-identical, cut its board cycles on 05.png at w = 4 by 7.6%, from 202.8 to 187.4 per pixel (a second relabelling: 7.0%), and the window count plus each layer's fill and drain (min(load, last window's compute) + 3 + the outputs in the last group of P, read off the earlier board runs), both pushed before the relabelled networks were exported and run, gave both totals to the cycle once the 12 reported input-stall cycles of one repeat were removed. The same count gives no saving on this network at w = 1 or 2, where its hidden windows stay input-bound, and 5.1–11.2% per image at w = 4 for the two relabellings on the test images not used to calibrate them.

**Shorter version (two sentences), if the page limit does not allow three.**

**Channel order at w = 4.** At w = 4 a window waits for the fullest of the four tap banks, which in 32-channel layers hold whole channels by index mod 4 [24]; relabelling the B-32 ReLU network's hidden channels offline, folded into the adjacent layers' weights [25], cut its board cycles on 05.png by 7.0% and 7.6% for two relabellings (202.8 to 188.5 and 187.4 per pixel) with bit-identical output, as the window count plus each layer's fill and drain had predicted. The same count gives no saving on this network at w = 1 or 2, where its hidden windows stay input-bound.
